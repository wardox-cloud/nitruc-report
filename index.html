Reading the newsletter honestly

Opens are real, many clicks are machines

Unique clicks per link
Your website, nitruc.io · 1,325
Reply by email link · 1,337
Footer link Kit adds itself · 1,282

People rarely click a footer link as often as the main one. An even split like this usually means company email security scanners opened every link, so replies are the number to watch.

701 bounced, 4 spam complaints

On the Kit account. Kit can restrict accounts with high bounce rates, so bounced and old addresses come out before the Salary Outlook goes.

Kit's permission rule

Kit's terms ask that every subscriber has agreed to receive email. Tear sheets are CRM contacts, so we suggest a clear subscribe or unsubscribe line and a signup link in each issue.

What we learned

Six things that shape the next month

A number beats a pitch

The proof-point Message 1 got 4 replies to 0. It becomes the default.

Warm beats cold

Your connections reply at 14 to 25% against under 3% cold, so warm lists get refreshed first.

LinkedIn invites are the bottleneck

Your weekly limit runs out by Tuesday. We share it across campaigns and move more volume to email.

Replies, not clicks

Newsletter clicks are inflated by scanners. Replies and calls are what we report on.

Protect nitruc.io

Bulk sends go from separate domains, and bounced addresses come out of Kit before each send.

Bullhorn needs the API

Its screens won't export tear sheets cleanly. The API is live, so lists come straight across.
